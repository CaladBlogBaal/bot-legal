Privacy Policy for Victorique

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and sharing of information by Victorique, a Discord bot developed and operated by caladblogbaal. By using Victorique, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Victorique may store or process information associated with your use of the Bot, including your Discord user ID, username, server ID, server settings, and command-related information. Depending on the features you use, this may include virtual credits, cooldowns, fishing inventories, catches, custom tags, and other game-related data.

How We Use Your Information

We use information to operate and maintain Victorique, respond to commands, save game progress and server settings, provide Bot features, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and improve functionality.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Discord, our hosting provider, or external services when necessary to operate the Bot. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the Bot, its users, or our legal rights.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Victorique's features, maintain its operation, or comply with legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of your information, subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction. Victorique uses a PostgreSQL database and is hosted on third-party infrastructure. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published in the Victorique GitHub repository. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect is subject to the revised policy and applicable law.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or wish to request access to or deletion of your information, please contact the developer through the Victorique GitHub repository.

Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 25 October 2026.
